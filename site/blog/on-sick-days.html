--- title: "Why are sick days so depressing?" date: 2026-11-17 tags: ["life", "musings", "work"] description: "Some reflections on sick days, angst." ---

Being sick is absolutely the worst, file that among the hottest takes of my career, in other news receiving a package in the mail is like a present! And other relatable colloquia. But seriously, what's the deal with being sick?

Apart from the obvious, that fighting an infection, and managing your discomfort is hard, why are sick days so depressing? Like yeah, my throat hurts and I've got body aches and chills, but my hands can still type my brain can still think .. mostly, more on that later.. why can't I motivate myself to do anything?

The brain is a fickle and easily influenced by our environment and bodily .. um .. status? For example if you are sleep deprived, or very hungry, you might find it difficult indeed to focus on that API you are trying to design. Similarly if you are in a polluted, distracting environment, reading a few paragraphs feels like nails on the chalkboard agony in slow motion. It's the same while we are sick but for me I have a hard time figuring that out? Maybe not figuring that out but definitely in accepting this. I think oh I'm sick so I can just kick back and relax but my job is to literally sit around physically relaxed and put my brain through mental exercises from reading massive AI generated PR diffs to designing systems and solutions to problems that have one page of google results. Unlike those other things where it feels obvious why your productivity is ground to a halt, being sick has no immediate solution and it's so boring!

Seriously why the boredom, it feels way too hard to motivate oneself when you're sick. I know some folks take the time to binge a TV show or watch movies, play video games, but I find that I either cannot focus on these things for long enough to fill the day or I get frustrated that I'm not being productive enough and try to task switch endlenssly in hopes of finding something satisfying. So this led me to actually look it up and apparently having a mental slowdown while sick is a totally normal part of the experience. I knew that you feel physically fatigued as a response to not perform strenuous activies that would delay the healing process. What I did not know was that this actually extended to our ability to focus, think clearly, and have motivation to complete tasks. This explains the intense feeling of enui that accompanies any good cold that I suffer.

This ties in to an idea that I don't think was very well covered in traditional Health curriculum, that the body is a whole system which includes the mind, or put differently: the mind is a part of the body! DUH! But it's so obvious I think it's easy to forget, we expect to go to work in the early morning tired, drinking lots of coffee, working long uninterrupted hours, and for all of that to be fine. And the classic advice of 'take a walk', 'get plenty of sleep', 'eat a balanced diet', really do improve your mental capacity to focus and do work. Who would have thought. This reframe helped me be okay with sick days. Yes they are boring because I can't do any of the activities I usually do to rest my mind, like biking or climbing, and my brain is actively throttling my clock speeds, all the while I'm left to lie on the couch watching 2-3 mins of the most interesting Youtube recommended videos on my feed. It's temporary, and there is no immediate cure, just time, and good rest.
